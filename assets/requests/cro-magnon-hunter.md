## Scope

Create one complete Cro-Magnon player character candidate for the existing
five-player prehistoric browser game. Include an embedded texture, a working
humanoid skin and skeleton, and all eight animations below. This is an original
fictional game character. The game's third-person shoulder camera is the main
view; inspect the back, shoulders, hair, hips and feet as carefully as the face.

This instruction authorizes one successful candidate only. Follow the sibling
repository's coordinator, serial execution, frozen request, candidate numbering,
immutable output and retry rules. Resume a failed pending candidate. Do not start
another successful candidate, promote to `geometry/accepted.glb`, publish, or
perform Git remote operations. Do not replace the live game's files from the
modeling session. Codex will review and integrate the delivered candidate.

## Appearance and conditioning image

- One adult human with natural human proportions, an athletic build, a readable
  face, dark brown tied-back hair, and a short beard. Avoid a hunched caricature.
- A practical, fully clothed prehistoric adventurer: a fitted brown hide tunic
  ending above the knees, a leather belt, modest fur around the shoulders, and
  hide ankle footwear. Keep elbows, hands, knees and feet readable. Clothing is
  fictional art direction, not an archaeological reconstruction claim.
- Use warm natural skin, brown hide, dark hair and nonmetallic materials. Give
  skin, hair and hide appropriate roughness; preserve the source albedo.
- Keep the shoulder silhouette compact enough for a close camera. Avoid large
  backpacks, long loose capes, floor-length skirts or hair covering the hands.
- A small shoulder or belt accent should support the game's five player colours.
  If practical, isolate that source-derived region as a material named
  `TribeAccent`, with a neutral light base suitable for tinting. Do not recolour
  the entire skin or hide to identify a player. Record any limitation explicitly.
- Empty hands. Weapons, resources and scenery are separate future model tasks;
  do not merge a spear, axe, stone, berry, floor, pedestal or background into this
  character. Do not add procedurally constructed visible props.
- Prepare a fully framed reference in a symmetrical relaxed A-pose, with arms
  separated from the torso, hands readable, legs apart and both feet visible.
  Use a neutral separable background and a modest front three-quarter angle.
  Keep perspective distortion low. Retain source image, prompt, conditioning
  image, matte, settings and hashes.
- Use ZImage by default. Use an equivalent image generator only if permitted by
  the repository policy and actually available. If image generation is blocked,
  report the blocker; never substitute a procedural body or an unrelated asset.

## Required lineage and quality

Follow `MODELING_WORKFLOW_POLICY.md` and `docs/SEQUENTIAL_MODEL_GENERATION.md`:
source image → TRELLIS-2 dense reconstruction → dense geometry inspection →
conservative denoising if necessary → silhouette-preserving reduction → bounded
repair, UVs and albedo bake → rig, animation and GLB → exact-output QA.

Use the highest practical reconstruction fidelity supported by the local
resources. Determine polygon density from the subject and matched-view quality;
there is no user-specified triangle or file-size ceiling. Record dense and final
counts, bytes, settings, topology metrics and any smoothing displacement.
Preserve face, fingers or readable hand shapes, elbows, knees, boot soles,
clothing folds and the rear silhouette. Reject fused limbs, accidental holes,
granular skin, floating fragments and materially worse reduced geometry.

Blender may repair, reduce, bake, rig, animate and package the TRELLIS geometry.
Do not build the visible character from primitives, curves, BMesh lofts or
Geometry Nodes. The existing procedural CRO-MAGNON character is a placeholder
and must not become the production mesh or modeling template.

  pedestal, automatic centring wrapper, global rotation track or world travel.

  `Hips`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Shoulder.L/R`, `UpperArm.L/R`,
  `LowerArm.L/R`, `Hand.L/R`, `UpperLeg.L/R`, `LowerLeg.L/R`, `Foot.L/R`,

- Weight every deforming vertex. Use at most four nonzero influences, normalise
  their sum to one, and remove incorrect opposite-side limb influences.
- Verify skeleton hierarchy, joint indices and inverse bind matrices after GLB
  re-import. Inspect bent elbows/knees, shoulders, wrists, hips and ankles.
- Reference the implementation in
  `models/cool-cyberpunk-woman-rigged/workflow/batch-010/run-01/attempt-01/`

## Animation contract

Produce all clips at 30 fps. The following names and loop types are required;
durations are initial authoring targets and may change to improve the movement.
Record final durations and intended locomotion speed in the delivery report.

| Exact clip name | Loop | Target duration | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Idle_Loop` | Yes | 4 s | Calm breathing and subtle weight shift; feet stay planted. |
| `Walk_Loop` | Yes | 1 s | Full-body walk with clear alternating foot contacts. |
| `Run_Loop` | Yes | 0.7 s | Full-body run with coordinated hips, arms, knees and feet. |
| `Gather` | No | 1.5 s | Bend at hips/knees, reach toward a resource, return to standing. |
| `Craft` | No | 2 s | Work with both hands in front of the body, then return to rest. |
| `Give` | No | 1.2 s | Offer with hands forward, pause briefly, withdraw. |
| `Eat` | No | 1.8 s | Bring one hand to the mouth with clearance, then lower it. |
| `Wave` | No | 2 s | Raise a hand, make a readable greeting, lower it. |

All movement is in-place. Root translation and heading remain fixed; local hip
motion is allowed. Loop endpoints must match in pose and maintain a convincing
cadence. Single actions start and finish near idle so they can blend cleanly.
Avoid ground penetration, collapsing triangles and hand/body intersections.
Empty-hand actions should remain readable without adding unrequested props.

The current authoritative game speed is `WORLD.speed = 8` world units/second,
with `moving` and `facing` in the player state. There is no walk/run toggle yet.
Author both clips and document their intended speed/stride so Codex can match
playback or make an explicit movement change; do not imply that animation alone
solves foot sliding at the current speed.
