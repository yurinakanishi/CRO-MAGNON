Privacy and analytics settings

Created by yuri. Updated October 23, 2026.

Game saves

Solo progress is autosaved in this browser's IndexedDB. Your name, controls, audio, language and other settings are stored in localStorage. Game saves and the name you enter are not sent to Google Analytics. Clearing this site's browser data also removes your progress and settings.

Optional analytics

The public site loads Google Analytics 4 only if you allow it. Page visits, engagement time and device/browser information are sent to Google, using cookies to measure usage. Advertising signals and personalization are disabled. URL query strings, fragments, player names, inventory and save data are excluded from analytics. Only the referrer's origin is used; its page path and query string are excluded.

You can play without allowing analytics. Change your choice below at any time. Declining stops further measurement and removes this site's Analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in this browser. This does not delete data already sent to Google.
